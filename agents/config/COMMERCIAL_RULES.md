# Commercial rules

## Two distinct revenue models

### RESALE
White Carrara purchases goods and resells them. Track:
- supplier purchase cost
- processing / packing
- freight and insurance
- import duties and taxes where relevant
- sales price
- gross margin
- revenue recognition confirmed by accountant

### BROKERAGE
White Carrara introduces or facilitates a transaction and earns a commission under a written agreement. Track:
- principal supplier
- buyer
- commission basis and rate
- trigger for commission entitlement
- payment due date
- whether White Carrara acts as disclosed intermediary or agent

Do not report the full underlying goods value as White Carrara revenue when only a commission is earned. Accounting and legal treatment must be confirmed by qualified professionals.

## Fulfilment
- OWN_STOCK: stock physically controlled by White Carrara.
- SHOWROOM_STOCK: sample/display stock; not automatically saleable inventory.
- SUPPLIER_DIRECT: supplier confirms stock and fulfils directly.

Every availability statement must include source, timestamp, quantity, unit, lot identifier and expiry/recheck policy.

## Approval gates
Human approval is required for supplier purchase orders, customer contracts, payment instructions, credit, non-standard discounts, stock reservations, binding quotes outside approved limits, and any action with material legal or financial consequences.
